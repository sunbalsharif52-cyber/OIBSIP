# DataAnalytics-L1-EDARetailSales

## *Project Overview*
This project contains the Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of retail sales data. The goal of this analysis is to extract meaningful insights from sales records, understand customer purchasing patterns, and identify product performance trends to support data-driven business decisions.

## *Key Objectives*
- *Data Cleaning:* Handled data types, removed symbols, and verified date formats for analysis.
- *Data Visualization:* Created visual representations of sales trends, revenue by category, and correlation analysis.
- *Business Insights:* Provided actionable recommendations based on top-performing product categories.

## *Tools and Libraries*
- *Language:* Python  
- *Libraries:* Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn  
- *Environment:* Jupyter Notebook  

## *Key Insights*
- *Sales Trends:* A positive growth trend observed between February and March 2024.
- *Top Performers:* 'Mountain Bikes' and 'Road Bikes' are the primary revenue drivers.
- *Operational Insight:* A strong relationship exists between order quantity and total order value.
